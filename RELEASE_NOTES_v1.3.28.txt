SkyBarech ERP v1.3.28

- Android sales synced to Desktop now appear in Today Sales using the shop's local calendar date.
- The seven-day sales chart and cash closing use the same local-date logic.

- Android Laptop entry and stock cards now show laptop-specific specifications.
- Saving an Android laptop now returns to Laptop stock instead of Mobile Accessories.
- Laptop specifications are retained during Android/Desktop cloud sync.
