# SkyBarech ERP product requirements

SkyBarech ERP manages mobile-shop inventory, sales, repairs, customers, purchases, staff and receipts on Android and Windows.

## Required behavior

- One owner account may sign in on Android and Desktop using the same 4-digit PIN.
- Both clients sync products, stock, sales and permitted staff data through the Hostinger API.
- POS must make products, cart, total, review, save and receipt printing visible on a phone-sized screen.
- Android supports compact in-app IMEI/barcode scanning and paired Bluetooth ESC/POS thermal printing.
- Only server-approved roles may create or operate staff accounts.
